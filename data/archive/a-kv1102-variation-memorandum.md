Variation to clause 9 (delivery windows), master supply agreement.

1. The existing window is 48 hours from the order acknowledgement.
2. The supplier has requested 96 hours. Operationally this is understood to be
   acceptable at the Midlands depots and unacceptable at Bristol.
3. A split window is available: 96 hours generally, 48 hours for Bristol, drawn
   as a schedule rather than a change to the operative clause.
4. If a single widened window is accepted, the liquidated damages at clause 14
   fall away proportionately. This should be priced, not conceded.

This memorandum remains current until the agreement is re-executed.
