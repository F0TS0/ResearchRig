Hull and machinery policy, clause 6 (exclusions).

Excluded: loss or damage attributable to wear, tear, gradual deterioration or
corrosion existing prior to the inception of the risk.

Note that the exclusion bites on the *cause*, not the *manifestation*. Where
grounding damage and pre-existing corrosion are both present, the burden of
establishing that the corrosion was causative rests with insurers, not with the
assured. This is the point on which the claim is likely to turn.
